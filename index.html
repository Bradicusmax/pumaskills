What PUMA does

Practical beats theoretical.

Modern AI is advancing faster than most companies can absorb it. PUMA exists around the application layer: finding where new capabilities become genuinely useful, then turning them into workflows, tools, products, and operating systems.

Projects may include AI agents, intelligent workflows, automation, digital products, internal systems, software experiments, and other technology-enabled businesses.

Operating idea

Technology should earn its place.

PUMA is intentionally broad. AI changes too quickly to build a company around one model, interface, or trend. The durable question is whether a new capability creates measurable utility.

That principle guides the work: experiment quickly, keep what proves useful, discard what does not, and build around the parts that survive contact with reality.
